The settings, as you shaped them today

Clickable prototype in CUE’s own styles: simple cleanup and set up with an AI agent.

Decided

  • Speech: Parakeet Ultra for both languages, language from the speech, as beforeKeyboard-layout modes and GigaAM are off the table for now.
  • Cleanup has two choices: AI in the cloud with your key, or a model on this computer
  • All eight cloud services stay, shown as tiles with their key status, models and prices
  • Claude Haiku is the recommended cloud model, for Russian and English
  • Gemma is out as the local model
  • A “Set up with an AI agent” button copies a prompt for Claude, Codex or another agentThe agent edits settings; it never sees your keys.
