# Habits

Part 5 of bringing the approved main window (design stage 5) into the app: when you correct the same thing after a
dictation again and again, CUE learns it as a habit, says so once, and from then on asks the cleanup to write it your
way. Habits build on the correction watch of parts 3 and 4 (docs/specs/2026-11-02-corrections.md).

Chosen on 04.11.2026 from the live variants: section A ("as in the mockup") and notice A ("card").

## What the user sees

1. In Telegram CUE pastes "Скинь файл." You change it to "скинь файл". Twice more, on other days, the same.
2. After the third time a tray card grows out of the Drop, like the other cards (design stage 3): the habit icon in
   green, **New habit** / «Новая привычка», the body "In Telegram — lower case and no full stop. CUE writes it this way
   from now on." / «В Telegram — с маленькой буквы и без точки. Дальше CUE пишет так сам.», and **Undo** / «Отменить».
   The card closes by itself after 7 s (the ring on its cross); hovering pauses it. "Undo" turns into "Removed" /
   «Убрано» (the label morphs by shared letters), the habit is forgotten and the card closes a moment later.
3. From then on, a dictation in Telegram is cleaned up without the capital and the full stop.
4. The main window gets a **Habits** / «Привычки» section after Dictionary, and the header link «Привычки»:
   - the heading with the count, and the switch **Learn from me** / «Учиться у меня» at the heading's right (it moves
     here from the settings tray's General page);
   - the hint "How you correct text after a dictation. When a correction repeats, CUE remembers it and writes it that
     way itself. Kept only on this computer." / «Как вы правите текст после диктовки. Когда правка повторяется, CUE
     запоминает её и дальше пишет так сам. Хранится только на этом компьютере.»;
   - one row per habit, the newest first: the rule (13 px, medium); under it the example "before" (struck, muted) →
     "after" (soft green tint) · where ("везде" with the globe icon, or «в Telegram») · how many times («3 раза»); a
     switch at the right; the trash icon «Забыть» appears on hover;
   - an empty list says "Nothing yet. CUE will notice when you correct the same thing a few times." / «Пока ничего. CUE
     заметит, если вы несколько раз поправите одно и то же.»;
   - **Forget all** / «Забыть всё» at the bottom; the first press turns it into "Press again" / «Нажмите ещё раз», the
     second forgets every habit;
   - with "Learn from me" off, the list is muted (the rows' text takes `--muted`, never opacity: contrast stays ≥ 4.5:1)
     and nothing is learned or applied;
   - with no cleanup connected, a line under the hint: "Habits work when the cleanup is on." / «Привычки работают,
     когда включена очистка.»
5. In the History, an entry whose text shows a habit's effect (see "Applied") lists it under "as you said it": a small
   chip with the habit icon and its short name («без точки»).

A new row slides in with the highlight of a new dictionary chip; a removed one folds away; switching a habit off mutes
its row in place. Every change morphs (design family motion rules). Texts are in English and Russian.

## The switch

`learn.enabled` (parts 3 and 4) covers habits too: off, no field is read, nothing is counted or learned, and no habit
goes into a request. The row on the General page is removed; the switch lives in the Habits heading.

## How a habit is found

**Kinds** (`cue-core::habits`, pure). From the pasted text and the corrected span (the watch of parts 3 and 4), CUE
takes the corrections of these kinds, one correction can hold several:

- **End mark:** the last sentence mark removed or added (`.`, `!`, `?`, `…`).
- **First letter:** the first letter's case changed.
- **Replacement:** a run of one to three words replaced by another, compared without case and outer punctuation; not a
  dictionary candidate (`corrections::candidates` takes those), and not a change of case or punctuation only.
- **Digits:** number words replaced by digits ("двенадцать процентов" → "12 %").
- **Dash:** a hyphen between words replaced by a dash ("клиенты - в основном" → "клиенты — в основном"); the one
  punctuation change counted, as the others are too noisy to be habits.

Each kind has a **form**: the kind with its detail (`EndMark { removed: '.' }`, `FirstLetter { lower }`,
`Replacement { from: "здравствуйте", to: "добрый день" }`, `Digits`, `Dash`). The form is counted by its **hash**, never by its
text.

**The hash's secret** (decided 06.11.2026). The hash mixes in a random secret of this installation, made on first use
(32 random bytes) and kept in Windows Credential Manager under the service "CUE", beside the connections' keys, never in
settings.json. Without it, a copy of settings.json does not let anyone guess a replacement's words by hashing common
words. When the secret is missing or malformed, CUE makes a new one and drops `habits.seen` and `habits.declined`
(their hashes no longer match); habits already learned keep working, because their forms keep their details and their
hashes and ids are computed again with the new secret. Under the secret kept, a habit whose id was made otherwise (a
settings.json restored or brought from another computer) gets its id made again, and a set counted under the old id
follows it. When the store cannot be read for another reason, nothing is counted
or declined until the next start, and nothing is overwritten.

**Opposite forms** (decided 06.11.2026). Each form has an opposite where one exists: an end mark removed ↔ added,
first letter to lower case ↔ to capital, a replacement A → B ↔ B → A. Digits and Dash have none. Two habits with
opposite forms never live together in one app (or one "везде" habit and an app's habit): see "Changed".

**Counting.** `habits.seen` in settings.json: `[{ "app": "telegram.exe", "form": "<hash>", "n": 2, "last": "2026-11-04" }]`
— no text. A form counts at most once per dictation. A count not seen for 30 days is dropped; at most 300 counts are
kept (the oldest go first). `app` is the dictation's app as the history records it.

**Learning.** When a form reaches 3 in one app, CUE makes a habit. Forms of one app that reach 3 in the same dictation
make one habit together (lower case and no full stop: one rule, one row, one card); its applied check passes when all
its forms show:

1. It asks the cleanup connection for the rule: one short request with the kind, the example (the corrected words with
   up to two words around them on each side, at most 40 characters on each side) and the app's name. The answer is a
   rule as a sentence in the interface language, at most 80 characters ("В Telegram — с маленькой буквы и без точки"),
   and a short name of one to three words ("без точки"). Nothing else goes in the request. The example is a few
   words of a text the cleanup already received when it was dictated; the README's privacy part says so.
2. Without a cleanup connection, or when the answer does not come in 5 s or is not usable (empty, too long, more than
   one line), the rule and the short name come from a template for the kind ("In Telegram: no full stop at the end",
   «В Telegram: без точки в конце»; a replacement: «Писать «добрый день» вместо «здравствуйте»»).
3. The habit is saved and the card is shown when the Drop is at rest (as a dictionary suggestion: idle, no tray card,
   no answer, not paused, not hidden; it waits otherwise). One card at a time; a dictionary suggestion and a habit card
   never show together, the one found first goes first.

A form whose habit was undone or forgotten goes into `habits.declined` (hashes only) and is never learned again; "Forget
all" clears that list too.

**Wider** (changed 06.11.2026). Each app has its own counts. A habit of one app becomes "везде" only when, in another
app, all of its forms come together in one dictation three times (the habit's whole set is counted as one more entry
of `habits.seen`, under the habit's id). Then its app is cleared, it takes the id of "везде" and its count adds up;
the counts of its set go, in every app (none follows the new id); a habit of the same forms in another app folds into
it, its count added. No card, the row changes in place. A form of the habit seen alone in the other app counts for that app as any form does, and can
make that app's own habit.

**Changed** (decided 06.11.2026). When a correction holds the opposite of a form of a habit that is on and applies in
that app, it counts like any form. When that opposite reaches 3, the old habit goes and the new one takes its place:
the old one is removed (not declined, so it can be learned again), the new one is learned as usual (rule request,
template), and its card says **Habit changed** / «Привычка изменилась» with the new rule; its "Undo" brings the old
habit back as it was and declines the new forms. A card whose draft only added to a habit (see "The habit") learned
none: its "Undo" brings that habit back as it was, with the habits it replaced, and declines nothing. A change of a
habit whose card still waits takes that card's place: the waiting card goes, and the new one's "Undo" brings back what
was there before both. A "везде" habit is changed only by opposites from any app reaching 3
in one app; the change then applies everywhere.

**The habit** (`habits.list` in settings.json, the newest first, at most 50):
`{ "id", "forms": [{ "kind", … }], "rule", "short", "before", "after", "app": "telegram.exe" | null, "n", "on": true }`.
`forms` are the kinds of correction it learned together, each with its detail: `{ "kind": "endMark", "mark": ".",
"removed": true }`, `{ "kind": "firstLetter", "lower": true }`, `{ "kind": "replacement", "from": "здравствуйте", "to":
"добрый день" }`, `{ "kind": "digits" }`, `{ "kind": "dash" }`; `id` is made from the forms, the app and the secret
(the hash of the forms' hashes and the app, none for "везде"), so the habits of the same forms in two apps are two
habits. A replacement's words
are words of its example. `before`/`after` are the example, at most 40 characters each; it is the only text of a
correction ever written to disk (decided 04.11.2026). It is never logged; the log gets "habit learned" with the kinds
and numbers. Two drafts of the same forms in one app (the second reached 3 while the first waited for its rule) make
one habit: the second adds to the first, with no second card; so does a draft whose forms' habit became "везде"
meanwhile. A habit of another app is never added to: two apps' drafts of the same forms make two habits.

## Applied

**Into the request.** The habits that are on and belong to the dictation's app or to "везде" go into the cleanup request
as a block after the dictionary block, one rule per line ("The user's habits; follow them unless the speech clearly asks
otherwise: …"). With none, the request is exactly the one without habits. Translation and "Ask" take no habits.

**The sanity check.** The words a sent replacement writes ("добрый день") count as said in the cleanup's sanity check,
like the dictionary's terms, for that request only: otherwise a short phrase it rewrote ("здравствуйте анна" → "Добрый
день, Анна!") falls back to the raw text. They are never added to the dictionary; a translation's check takes none.

**What the History shows.** After the cleanup, CUE checks each sent habit against the result: an end mark is absent (or
present), the first letter has the case, the replacement's "after" is there and its "before" is not, the digits are
there. Only the habits whose effect the cleanup brought, in the text and not in the text as heard (the recognizer may
have written the digits itself), are recorded on the entry (`habits: [{ "id", "short" }]`) and shown as chips. A
forgotten habit's chip shows its short name as it was.

## Settings changes

`habitOn { id, on }`, `habitForget(id)`, `habitsForget` (all, and the declined list). The core sends `cue://settings`
to the main window after it learns, widens or forgets a habit. `SettingsView.habits` lists them as the section needs
(id, rule, before, after, app name for display, n, on).

## The card

A new tray kind, `newHabit`, carrying the rule. Its "Undo" is the card's action: the core forgets the habit and adds its
form to the declined list. The card has the icon of the habit (a pen with a plus, drawn in the window's icon style) in
the `ok` tone.

## Out of scope

Editing a habit's rule by hand, a habit for one person or one chat inside an app, habits for "Ask" answers, learning
from corrections made with the keyboard outside a dictation's paste, showing the declined list.

## Testing

- Core: the kinds and forms from pasted/corrected pairs (each kind, several in one correction, names left to the
  dictionary, case-only and punctuation-only changes); hashing is stable; counting, the 30-day drop and the cap;
  learning at 3, widening, declining; the template rules; the applied check per kind; the request block (with none,
  the request is unchanged); settings changes and `SettingsView.habits`; Debug and logs print no text.
- Runtime: the rule request's body holds only the kind, the example and the app name; the 5 s fallback.
- Pages: the section (rows, switch, forget with hover, forget all with the second press, muted when off, the no-cleanup
  line, empty state), the card (7 s, hover pause, Undo → Removed), the History chips.
- Live: Telegram (end mark and first letter), Почта (a replacement), Документы (dash), both themes.
