# Today: the main window's first section, and the header's links

Part 1 of bringing the approved main window (design stage 5) into the app: Today, then Dictionary, corrections,
dictionary suggestions, Habits and the remaining settings, each with its own spec.

## What the user sees

At the top of the main window, above History:

- **A sentence with the numbers in it.** "Today — **2 340** words. That's **58** minutes without the keyboard." The
  numbers are Bebas Neue (34 px), the words around them the interface font (13 px, weight 600).
- **30 bars**, one per local day, today last and dark, the rest muted. A bar's height is its day's words against the
  largest day of the 30 (at least 2 px). Hovering a bar shows a dark tooltip: the number (Bebas) and the date
  ("Sat, October 31").
- **One muted line** (12 px): "**140** words a minute · **6** days in a row · **48 210** words in all", numbers in Bebas.
- **A milestone**, once: when the total passes a round number (1 000, 5 000, 10 000, 25 000, 50 000, 100 000, then
  every 100 000), a dark pill under the line, "**100 000** words — about 400 pages of a book" (250 words a page), and
  a burst of lines from the number. Shown once per milestone; the pill stays for the rest of that day.

The header gets links between the mark and the gear: **Today · History**. A click scrolls the page to the section
(smoothly, at once with reduced motion); the link of the section under the header is darker. Dictionary and Habits
join the links when their sections exist.

Texts are in English and Russian, like the rest of the window. Numbers are written the way each language writes them
(`Intl.NumberFormat`): "2 340" with a no-break space in Russian, "2,340" in English.

## What is counted

- **Words**: the words of the text CUE pasted or left in the clipboard for a dictation or a translation (outcomes
  `inserted` and `no_field`). "Ask", cancelled, empty, failed and discarded attempts do not count. A word is a
  whitespace-separated run that holds a letter or a digit.
- **Minutes without the keyboard**: today's words divided by 40, the typical typing speed in words a minute, rounded.
- **Words a minute**: the counted words of the last 30 days divided by their audio minutes, rounded; nothing to show
  (no audio) hides that part of the line.
- **Days in a row**: the run of local days with at least one counted word that ends today, or yesterday while today
  has none yet; 0 hides that part.
- **In all**: every counted word since CUE first wrote usage.csv.

## Where the numbers come from

usage.csv already keeps one row per attempt forever, with no text. It gets two columns at its end:

- `words`: the count above (0 for rows that count nothing);
- `day`: the local date of the row, `YYYY-MM-DD` (its `ts` is UTC).

The header of an existing file is not rewritten; rows of three widths read: 13 columns (before the modes), 16 (before
these columns), 18. A row without `words` gets an estimate: its audio seconds times the speaking speed measured on the
rows that have `words` (150 words a minute while there are none), only for the outcomes that count. A row without
`day` takes the date of its UTC `ts` moved by the local offset now. The monthly cost summary is unchanged.

The core gets `stats::compute(csv, today, offset_minutes) -> Stats`, a pure function over the file's text:

```text
Stats { today_words, saved_minutes, days: [ { date, words } ; 30, oldest first ], wpm: Option, streak, total,
        milestone: Option<u64> (the largest round number ≤ total) }
```

The command `stats_get` (main window only) reads usage.csv, calls it and answers; a missing file is all zeros. The
window asks on start and again a moment (1 s) after each new history entry, and when the date changes.

Which milestone was shown, and on which local date, is kept in the window's localStorage (`cue.milestone`,
`{"n":100000,"date":"2026-11-01"}`): a number it has not celebrated yet gets the burst and today's date; the pill shows
while the stored date is today. Clearing storage at most shows the last one again.

## Out of scope

Dictionary, corrections, Habits, the other settings (later parts). No per-app or per-mode breakdown, no export.

## Testing

- Core: word counting; parsing the three row widths; the estimate for old rows; days and the 30-day window across
  months; the streak with and without today, across a gap; wpm with no audio; milestones at and around the bounds.
- App: the row written for an inserted dictation carries its words and day; an Ask row carries 0.
- Window (vitest): the sentence, bars and line from a stand-in `stats_get`; a bar's tooltip; parts hidden at zero;
  the milestone pill and its once-only burst; the links scroll and mark the current section; Russian and English
  number formats.
- Preview in both themes against the design.
